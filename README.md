## 🎯 Business Problem Statement

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior in order to **increase sales, improve customer satisfaction, and build long-term customer loyalty**.

Management has observed changes in purchasing patterns across different **customer demographics, product categories, seasons, and purchasing behaviors**. They want to identify the key factors that influence customer purchasing decisions and repeat purchases, including **discounts, product reviews, seasonal trends, shipping preferences, and subscription behavior**.

The business needs a data-driven approach to answer the following overarching question:

> **"How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?"**

This project analyzes customer transaction data using **Python, PostgreSQL/SQL, and Power BI** to transform raw shopping data into actionable business insights.

---

## 📋 Project Deliverables

Clean, transform, and prepare the raw customer shopping dataset for further analysis.

Key activities include:

* Loading and exploring the dataset using Pandas
* Understanding data types and data distributions
* Identifying and handling missing values
* Standardizing column names
* Checking data consistency
* Creating meaningful derived features
* Preparing the cleaned dataset for database analysis

## 🔎 Key Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer the following questions:

1. Which customer demographic generates the highest revenue?
2. How does purchasing behavior differ between male and female customers?
3. Do subscribers spend more than non-subscribers?
4. Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?
5. Which products have the highest customer ratings?
6. Which products are most dependent on discounts?
7. How does discount usage affect customer spending?
8. Which products are most popular within each category?
9. Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
10. Which age groups contribute the most revenue?
11. Does shipping type influence average purchase value?
12. What strategies can improve customer retention and subscription adoption?
